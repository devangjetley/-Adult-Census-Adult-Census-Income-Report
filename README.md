Project Overview:
This project aims to gain insights into the adult census and income levels of adults using Microsoft Power BI. The analysis focuses on demographic data and income distributions to understand the characteristics of the adult population.

Key Insights:
•	Total Population: 32.56K with an average age of 38.58 years.
•	Age Distribution: The majority of the population falls within the 31-40 age range.
•	Occupational Insights: Most individuals are employed in craft and repair occupations, with a high school diploma being the most common educational attainment.
•	Marital Status: The predominant marital status is married, with a civilian spouse.
•	Workforce Composition: Most individuals belong to the private sector workforce.

Income Report:
•	Average Capital: $1.08K
•	Average Income: $87.30
•	Average Work Hours: 40.44 hours per week.
•	Individuals under 20 years earn less than $50K, while those aged 21-30 earn more than $50K.
•	In terms of work class, individuals in the private sector typically earn more than $50K.

Research Questions:
Q1) What are the demographic characteristics of the adult population?
Q2) How does income vary by age and occupation?
Q3) What trends can be observed in education and marital status among adults?

Tool Used:
•	Microsoft Power BI

Dataset Information:
The dataset includes demographic and income information for adults, providing a comprehensive view of various attributes such as age, occupation, education level, marital status, and income.

Conclusion:
The interactive dashboard developed through this project provides valuable insights into adult census data, allowing for enhanced data exploration with features like a country slicer. This analysis can inform policy decisions and business strategies by highlighting key demographic trends and income patterns.
